Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CapitalCurb AI by Hastradar.

What is CapitalCurb AI and how does it work?

CapitalCurb AI is an intelligent Android application developed by Hastradar Consultancy designed to curb financial leaks, categorize business and personal expenses, detect recurring subscription price hikes, and prepare monthly tax deduction audit reports without storing financial data on the cloud.

Does CapitalCurb AI require linking personal bank accounts or SMS access?

No. CapitalCurb AI prioritizes complete financial privacy. It does not require intrusive bank logins or cloud synchronizations. All ledger records and expense data are encrypted on your local Android device using on-device encrypted database storage.

What currencies does CapitalCurb AI support?

CapitalCurb AI natively supports all major global currencies including Indian Rupee (INR - ₹), US Dollar (USD - $), Euro (EUR - €), British Pound (GBP - £), UAE Dirham (AED), and Singapore Dollar (SGD), allowing multi-currency conversion for cross-border software subscriptions.

How does CapitalCurb AI help prevent subscription creep and unwanted charges?

The app continuously audits recurring monthly billing cycles, detects unauthorized micro-charges, and alerts you 48 hours prior to auto-renewals so you can cancel unused trials before incurring surprise credit card charges.

How do I back up my financial records when changing Android phones?

You can generate an encrypted local backup file directly from the Settings menu. Transfer this backup file via USB, local Wi-Fi, or your private cloud drive to your new Android device and restore your full history with one tap using your master passphrase.
