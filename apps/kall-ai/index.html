Five Technological Pillars of Kall AI

Engineered from the ground up for high-touch service businesses, doctors, and professionals who cannot afford to miss valuable leads.

Silent On-Device Call Screening Gate

Kall AI leverages Android's native RoleManager.ROLE_CALL_SCREENING to inspect incoming caller IDs locally in milliseconds. When auto-answer is enabled, the call is declined silently without your phone ever ringing.

  • Zero network latency on local decision
  • Respects weekly schedule windows

1-Tap Universal Carrier Forwarding

No memorizing arcane MMI codes. Kall AI provisions standard telecom conditional call forwarding (*67 / *61 / *62) for Airtel, Jio, Vi, and BSNL in one single tap with auto SIM slot selection.

  • Seamless dual-SIM support
  • Instant 1-tap deactivation anytime

Gemini Flash-Lite & Cloud Speech

Sub-second natural conversational flow. Callers speak freely in English, Hindi, or Hinglish; Kall AI transcribes with Google Cloud STT, generates context-aware replies via Gemini Flash-Lite, and speaks back in human tones.

  • Natural interruption handling
  • Function calling for catalog & slot booking

DPDPA 2023 Mandatory AI Disclosure

Every call begins with an unalterable verbal disclosure in the caller's language that they are speaking with an AI assistant. Every disclosure event is logged to immutable compliance audit records.

  • Indian DPDP Act 2023 statutory alignment
  • 100% Inbound only • Zero spam outbound

Actionable Summaries & High-Priority Push

As soon as a call completes, Kall AI extracts caller intent, contact numbers, ordered items or booked clinic slots, and pushes high-priority FCM notifications with full transcripts to your dashboard.

  • Structured JSON extraction (Retail, Clinic, Personal)
  • 1-Tap call-back button directly in app

Live Minute Balance & Play Store Billing

Never worry about hidden call charges. Monitor your real-time included and bonus minutes directly on your dashboard. Subscriptions and affordable top-up minute packs are securely managed via Google Play Billing.

  • Google Play In-App Purchases
