Quick Troubleshooting Tips

  • Disable Battery Optimization: On Xiaomi (MIUI/HyperOS), Samsung (OneUI), or OnePlus, exclude Wakeo from aggressive battery killers to ensure on-time ringing.
  • Exact Alarm Permission: Ensure Alarms & Reminders permission is allowed in Android Settings > Apps > Wakeo > Special Permissions.
  • Re-pairing Child Device: If pairing times out, simply tap Generate New Code on the parent dashboard to create a fresh 6-digit PIN.
