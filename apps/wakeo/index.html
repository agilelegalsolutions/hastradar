❓ Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Wakeo's features, safety, and privacy.

What happens if my child's phone has no Wi-Fi or cellular internet?
Wakeo is engineered with a decoupled audio silencing architecture. The alarm audio is silenced natively and immediately on the local device the instant the child snaps their selfie or solves the morning wake challenge. The photo and verification timestamp are queued locally and uploaded to the parent dashboard the moment internet connectivity is restored.
Can my child simply press a snooze button and go back to sleep?
No. Wakeo deliberately omits traditional snooze buttons. The ringing screen remains active over the lock screen until the child proves they are awake and out of bed by snapping a front-facing selfie or completing the 3-sun morning wake challenge.
How does Wakeo protect child privacy and selfie photos?
All selfie photos are transmitted over encrypted TLS connections and stored in private Google Firebase Cloud Storage protected by strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Photos are accessible solely to paired parents on their private dashboard. We never conduct facial biometric recognition, never share data with advertisers, and comply strictly with COPPA, GDPR, and India's DPDP Act 2023. Read our Privacy Policy for complete details.
Can I manage multiple children from a single parent account?
Yes! Wakeo features a built-in Multi-Child Switcher. Parents can link multiple child phones using individual 6-digit numeric pairing codes. You can customize each child's name, set independent schedules, and switch between child dashboards with a single tap.
Why does Wakeo ask for Exact Alarm and Foreground Service permissions?
Wakeo targets Android 16 (API 36). Modern Android versions place idle phones into battery-saving "Doze" mode which silences ordinary background timers. The Exact Alarm permission (USE_EXACT_ALARM) and Media Playback Foreground Service allow Wakeo to wake the screen and trigger loud, un-dismissible alarm audio precisely at the scheduled second.
How do I delete my family account and all stored photos?
You have complete control over your data. You can delete your account, paired child profiles, and all associated selfie photos directly within the app settings. Alternatively, you can submit a deletion request via our Account Deletion Page or email support@hastradar.com.
