Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CondiScan AI and your dietary privacy.

How does CondiScan AI evaluate food labels?
When you point your camera at an ingredient list, on-device OCR recognizes the text tokens in memory. Our rules engine parses INS and E-number additives, flags ultra-processed NOVA 4 indicators, and checks the ingredients against active dietary conditions configured for the selected family profile.
Does my camera or health data get sent to cloud servers?
No. Camera frames and text OCR are processed entirely in volatile device memory using Google Play Services ML Kit. All profiles and scan records are stored in a private sandboxed SQLite database on your device. We have zero user accounts and zero cloud telemetry.
Can I track different health profiles for my kids and parents?
Yes! CondiScan AI supports multi-member household profiles. You can configure individual profiles for Infants, Toddlers, Adults, or Seniors, each with their own conditions (e.g. Celiac, Diabetes, Hypertension, Clean-Label preference). Switching active profiles takes just one tap.
How is CondiScan AI optimized for Android?
CondiScan AI is engineered with Kotlin 2.0, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and CameraX. Release builds are minified with R8 ProGuard rules, reducing bundle size down to just 8.4 MB with 100% unit test coverage.
