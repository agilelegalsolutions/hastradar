Account & Data Deletion Policy

Transparent data control procedures for CondiScan AI users on Android

1. Zero Cloud Server Policy

No Cloud Accounts: CondiScan AI operates 100% on-device. We do not operate user registration servers, login portals, or cloud databases. Because no personal data or scan history is ever uploaded to Hastradar servers, there are zero cloud databases storing your personal data.

2. In-App Data Management & Erasure

You have full, autonomous control over all data saved on your device:

Method A: Clear All Scan History

  1. Open the CondiScan AI app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings / Preferences tab.
  3. Tap “Clear Scan History”.
  4. Confirm the prompt. All stored ingredient transcripts, scores, and timestamps are immediately purged from the local SQLite database.

Method B: Manage or Delete Household Profiles

  1. Open the Profiles tab from the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select the profile you wish to remove or edit.
  3. Tap Delete Profile to permanently erase the health condition preferences associated with that household member.

Method C: Complete Device Reset & Uninstallation

  • Clear App Storage via Android Settings: Go to Settings > Apps > CondiScan AI > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage. This immediately deletes all databases, SharedPreferences, and cache files.
  • Uninstalling the App: Drag and drop the CondiScan AI app icon to Uninstall. Android's OS security sandbox permanently removes all local application data, cryptographic keys, and cached models.

3. Data Deletion Support Request

If you have questions about our data safety practices or require verification of compliance with privacy regulations (including GDPR, CCPA, and DPDP Act), please contact us directly:

  • Support Email: condiscan.app@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: [Data Erasure Request] CondiScan AI
  • Expected Response Time: Within 48 business hours.
