❓ Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BrickTrack by HastRadar.

Is my financial data kept private? ▼
Yes! BrickTrack stores all your budget records, expenses, and contractor payments locally in your device's private SQLite database. If you enable Cloud Sync, backups go directly to your personal Google Drive account.
How does the AI Receipt OCR work? ▼
Snap a photo of your paper store bill or handwritten site diary note. BrickTrack sends the image over an encrypted HTTPS connection to Google Gemini AI to extract material names, quantities, and prices automatically.
How are purchases processed? ▼
All Pro subscriptions and lifetime passes are handled securely by official Google Play Billing API. You can restore your purchases at any time on new Android devices logged into your Google Play account.
