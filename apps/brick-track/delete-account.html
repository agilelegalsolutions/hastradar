Account & Data Deletion Request

Official Data Deletion Procedure for Google Play Store Policy Compliance

🔒 Privacy Commitment: BrickTrack stores your financial expense records locally on your device or in your personal Google Drive account.

1. Immediate In-App Local Data Deletion

To delete all construction logs, contractor records, and settings stored locally on your Android device:

  1. Open Android Settings on your phone.
  2. Go to Apps → BrickTrack.
  3. Tap Storage & Cache.
  4. Select Clear Storage (or Clear Data).
  5. Uninstalling the app will also permanently delete all local app data.

2. Deleting Google Drive Cloud Backup Files

If you enabled personal Google Drive Cloud Sync:

  1. Open the Google Drive website or mobile app.
  2. Search for the BrickTrack_Backups folder or SQLite database files.
  3. Select and permanently delete the folder.

3. Requesting Manual Data Removal Assistance

If you require further assistance regarding data removal, send an email to support@hastradar.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" or contact us via our corporate website at https://hastradar.com. Requests are fulfilled within 48 hours.
