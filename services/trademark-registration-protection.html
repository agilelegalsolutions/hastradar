Trademark Registration & Brand Protection

Safeguard your corporate logos, brand names, product titles, and slogans with Hastradar Consultancy's comprehensive IP prosecution and enforcement counsel.

End-to-End Trademark Services

Under the Indian Trade Marks Act, 1999, registration grants exclusive legal rights to your brand assets, preventing unauthorized commercial exploitation. Our trademark division leads clients through every milestone of IP protection:

1. Comprehensive Trademark Search & Clearance

Detailed search across 45 NICE classes, phonetic similarity checks, and risk analysis prior to official filing.

2. Drafting & Filing Application

Precise classification of goods & services, user affidavit drafting, and expedited online submission with the Indian Patent Office (IPO).

3. Examination Hearing & Opposition Advocacy

Responding to Section 9 & 11 examination objections, drafting counter-statements, and appearing before the Registrar for hearings.

4. Litigation & Passing-Off Suits

Filing injunction applications and infringement lawsuits in High Courts for domain theft and counterfeit sales.
